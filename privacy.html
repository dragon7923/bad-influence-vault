🎨 What Bad Influence Hobby keeps

Last updated 12 October 2026. Bad Influence Hobby is a free app for keeping track of your hobby paints, currently being tested by friends and family.

What is saved in your account

  • Your email address, so you can sign in.
  • What you put in the app: your paints (name, brand, range, how many, barcode, notes, type), your painting recipes and your shopping list.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name and profile picture address that Google passes along with your email.

That's all. There is no password to keep: you sign in with a code sent to your email, or with your Google account.

Where it is kept

  • In a database run by Supabase, in the United States.
  • The database itself only lets a signed-in person see and change their own rows. Other users can't see your paints.
  • The person who runs Bad Influence Hobby can reach the database to keep it working and fix problems.
  • Your sign-in code is emailed through Brevo, an email delivery service, which handles your email address to deliver it.

What is kept on your phone or computer

  • A copy of your lists and your sign-in, in the browser's own storage, so the app opens quickly and works with no connection.
  • Changes you make with no connection wait there until they can be sent.
  • Signing out removes the copy from that device.

The camera

Barcodes and paint labels are read on your own device. Photos are not uploaded or kept.

What Bad Influence Hobby does not do

  • No adverts, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your information is not sold or shared with anyone.

Other services the app loads from

Like any website, the services that deliver the app can see your device's internet address when it loads: GitHub (the app and its paint lists), and jsDelivr and unpkg (code the app uses). They are not sent your account or your paints.

Deleting your account

In the app, at the bottom of the Add page, choose Delete my account. Your account, paints, recipes and shopping list are removed from the database straight away, on every device. It can't be undone.

The older Google Sheet version

If you use the earlier version, called Paint Checker, that works from your own Google Sheet, your paints are in that sheet in your own Google account, not in the database described here.
